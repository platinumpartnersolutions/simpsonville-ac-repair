1. Every H2 must be a complete question or a clear declarative 
   statement — not a vague label.
   
   WRONG H2: "Our Services"
   RIGHT H2: "What Does AC Repair Cost in Simpsonville, SC?"

   WRONG H2: "About Us"  
   RIGHT H2: "Why Simpsonville Homeowners Choose Us for AC Repair"

2. Every answer that follows an H2 must start with a direct 
   response to the H2 — as if someone asked the H2 as a question.
   Google AI Overviews pull the first sentence after a heading.

   H2: "How Long Does AC Repair Take in Simpsonville?"

   1-3 hours from diagnosis to completion, with same-day 
   service available on most standard repairs."
   ← AI Overview will pull exactly this sentence.

     important facts a visitor needs]
